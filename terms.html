1. The service

Nestopia provides online tools for schools: student information, assessment, attendance, behaviour, planning, reports, inclusion, wellbeing, observations, communication with parents and inspection evidence. The parts a school can use depend on its plan.

2. Accounts

  • School staff join with their school’s code and must be approved by the school.
  • Parents create a parent account and link their child with the code or invite from the school.
  • Keep your password private, and switch on two-step log-in if you can. Tell your school straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

3. Using Nestopia responsibly

  • Use Nestopia only for school purposes.
  • Record information about children accurately, kindly and professionally.
  • Only look at information you need for your role. Views of sensitive records are logged.
  • Do not share student information outside Nestopia unless your school’s policies allow it.
  • Do not try to access information you are not allowed to see, or interfere with the service.

4. Safeguarding

Nestopia helps schools record and share concerns about children, but it does not replace a school’s safeguarding procedures. If a child is in immediate danger, act straight away and follow your school’s policy.

5. Messages with parents

Messages between parents and staff are kept in Nestopia, and school leaders can read them to keep children and staff safe.

6. Plans, trials and payment

New schools start with a free trial. After that, the school pays for its chosen plan as agreed with us. If payment is late, we may limit the service after telling the school. A school can cancel at the end of its subscription.

7. The school’s data

The school owns its information. We process it only to provide the service, as set out in the data processing agreement. When a school leaves, it can ask for a copy before its information is deleted.

8. Availability

We work hard to keep Nestopia running and secure, but we cannot promise it will always be available or free of errors. We back up information regularly.

9. Changes

We may improve Nestopia and update these terms. We will tell schools about important changes before they apply.
