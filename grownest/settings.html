Backup

Download a full copy of your school’s GrowNest data. We recommend doing this on the first school day of each month and saving it somewhere safe, such as your school’s Google Drive.

The Excel file has every teacher, observation, judgement, ticked statement, learning walk, appraisal, SEF write-up and photo record. The zip has the photo files, in a folder for each teacher.

Phases

Set up the phases your school uses in its SEF. GrowNest uses them to group observation evidence by phase. Each grade can belong to one phase only.
