Data processing agreementاتفاقية معالجة البيانات

Last updated: November 2026

This agreement is between the school (the data controller) and Nestopia / ThinkNest (the data processor). It explains how Nestopia handles personal information on the school’s behalf, in line with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) and good practice for children’s information.

1. What Nestopia does

Nestopia stores and processes the personal information of the school’s staff, students and parents only to provide the Nestopia service, and only on the school’s documented instructions (which include the way the school sets up and uses Nestopia).

2. Types of information and people

Staff, students and parents, as described in the privacy notice, including special categories such as health, special educational needs, wellbeing and safeguarding information where the school chooses to record them.

3. Nestopia’s commitments

  • Keep the information confidential. Only Nestopia team members who need access to provide support may access it, and only when asked by the school.
  • Protect it with appropriate security: encryption in transit, access rules in the database, two-step log-in for staff, and an access log for sensitive records.
  • Use only the service providers listed in the privacy notice (sub-processors), and tell the school before adding a new one.
  • Help the school respond to requests from staff, parents and students to see, correct or delete their information.
  • Tell the school without undue delay, and within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting its information, with what we know and what we are doing about it.
  • At the end of the service, give the school a copy of its information on request, then delete it within 30 days.
  • Give the school the information it reasonably needs to show it is meeting its own data protection duties.

4. The school’s commitments

  • Have a lawful reason to record the information, and tell staff and families how it is used (for example in the school’s own privacy notice).
  • Give staff access suited to their role, and remove staff who leave.
  • Choose a safeguarding lead and follow its own safeguarding procedures.

5. Where information is stored

Information is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider. If information is stored outside the UAE, we make sure it is protected to the standard required by UAE law. (Please confirm the hosting region in your Supabase project settings and add it here.)
