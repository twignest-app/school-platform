Last updated: November 2026

Nestopia (part of ThinkNest) is a school platform used by schools in the United Arab Emirates and beyond. This notice explains what information Nestopia holds, why, and how it is protected. It is written for school staff, parents and students.

ملخّص: المدرسة هي المسؤولة عن بيانات طلابها وموظفيها، ونستوبيا تعالجها نيابةً عنها فقط. لا نبيع البيانات ولا نستخدمها للإعلانات. يمكن لأولياء الأمور طلب الاطلاع على بيانات أطفالهم أو تصحيحها من خلال المدرسة.

1. Who is responsible for the information

Each school that uses Nestopia decides what information it records and how it is used. The school is the data controller. Nestopia is the data processor: we store and process information only to provide the service to the school, and only on the school’s instructions. The details are in our data processing agreement.

2. What information Nestopia holds

AboutInformation
School staffName, email, role, classes, and what they record in Nestopia (for example observations, comments, registers and messages).
StudentsName, student number, class and grade, assessment results and report comments, attendance, behaviour, and, where the school uses them: inclusion records and support plans, wellbeing check-ins and concerns.
ParentsName, email, the child they are linked to, conference bookings and messages with the school.

Some of this information is sensitive, for example information about a child’s special educational needs, wellbeing or safeguarding. Schools record it to support and protect children.

3. Why it is used

Only to run the school’s Nestopia: teaching and assessment, reports, attendance, support for students, safeguarding, communication with families, and school improvement. We never sell information, never use it for advertising, and never use children’s information to train artificial intelligence.

4. Who can see it

  • Staff see what their role needs: a class teacher sees their own students; school leaders see their whole school.
  • Safeguarding concerns are visible only to the school’s safeguarding lead(s) and school leaders.
  • Parents see only their own child, and only what the school chooses to share (never inclusion, wellbeing or safeguarding records).
  • The Nestopia team can access a school’s information only to provide support or fix a problem, when the school asks us to.

5. How it is protected

  • Information is encrypted when it travels over the internet, and stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) with access rules in the database itself, not just on the page.
  • Staff can switch on two-step log-in.
  • Every view and change of safeguarding, wellbeing and inclusion records is written in an access log that cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Emails are sent through our email provider (Resend). Safeguarding emails never include details.

7. How long it is kept

For as long as the school uses Nestopia, or as the school decides. When a school leaves, we delete its information within 30 days, unless the school asks us to give it a copy first.

8. Your rights

Parents, students and staff can ask to see, correct or delete their information. Please ask your school first, because the school controls the information. Parents can also download or delete their parent account from the parent portal by contacting the school.

9. Children

Students do not have Nestopia accounts. Teachers record information about them, and parents see it through the parent portal. The morning wellbeing check-in is done on a teacher’s screen in class.

10. Changes

If we change this notice in an important way, we will tell schools first.
