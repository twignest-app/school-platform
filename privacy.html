Nestopia (part of ThinkNest) is a school platform used by schools in the United Arab Emirates and beyond. This notice explains what information Nestopia holds, why, and how it is protected. It is written for school staff, parents and students.

1. Who is responsible for the information

Each school that uses Nestopia decides what information it records and how it is used. The school is the data controller. Nestopia is the data processor: we store and process information only to provide the service to the school, and only on the school’s instructions. The details are in our data processing agreement.

3. Why it is used

Only to run the school’s Nestopia: teaching and assessment, reports, attendance, support for students, safeguarding, communication with families, and school improvement. We never sell information, never use it for advertising, and never use children’s information to train artificial intelligence.

4. Who can see it

  • Staff see what their role needs: a class teacher sees their own students; school leaders see their whole school.
  • Safeguarding concerns are visible only to the school’s safeguarding lead(s) and school leaders.
  • Parents see only their own child, and only what the school chooses to share (never inclusion, wellbeing or safeguarding records).
  • Medical records are seen by staff who look after the child (their teachers, the nurse, the office and school leaders). Allergies and important medical notes also appear on registers, trip lists and at pick-up so the child is kept safe. Parents can see and update their own child’s record.
  • ID photos of people who collect a child are seen only by the parent who added them and by the staff the school allows to check IDs (usually school leaders and the office). Photos of the person are shown to front desk staff when a child is collected.
  • Applications are seen only by the school’s admissions staff. A family can see its own application through a private link.
  • Fees: invoices and payments are seen by the school’s finance or office staff and school leaders, and each family sees only its own.
  • Leave reasons are seen only by the person and the school leaders who approve leave. Colleagues only see that someone is away and who is covering.
  • The school decides which staff jobs can see or change each part of Nestopia, using an access grid. Teachers only ever see their own classes.
  • The Nestopia team can access a school’s information only to provide support or fix a problem, when the school asks us to.

5. How it is protected

  • Information is encrypted when it travels over the internet, and stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) with access rules in the database itself, not just on the page.
  • Staff can switch on two-step log-in.
  • Every view and change of safeguarding, wellbeing and inclusion records is written in an access log that cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Uploaded files (ID photos, application documents, Nestlings work) are kept in private storage. They are opened through short-lived links, never public web addresses.
  • The public application form only lets a family add to its own application; it cannot read anything else.
  • Emails are sent through our email provider (Resend). Safeguarding emails never include details, and emails never include medical details or ID photos.
  • Nestopia does not take card payments. It records payments the school has received, so no card or bank details are stored.

7. How long it is kept

For as long as the school uses Nestopia, or as the school decides. When a school leaves, we delete its information within 30 days, unless the school asks us to give it a copy first.

  • At the start of each school year, the school can archive the previous year’s report information. Archived records are kept so parents can still see earlier report cards, until the school deletes them.
  • Applications that do not lead to a place should be deleted by the school when they are no longer needed. We recommend no more than one year.
  • ID photos and pick-up details should be removed by the parent or school when they are no longer needed, and when a child leaves.
  • Fee records are kept for as long as the school needs them for its accounts.

8. Your rights

Parents, students and staff can ask to see, correct or delete their information. Please ask your school first, because the school controls the information. Parents can also download or delete their parent account from the parent portal by contacting the school.

9. Children

Students do not have email accounts or passwords. In Nestlings, the student portal for Pre-K to Grade 5, a child signs in with a code from their teacher and can only see their own class and their own work. Teachers check posts before they appear (unless the school turns this off), and each child’s work is seen only by their teachers and their own parents. Teachers record all other information, and parents see it through the parent portal. The morning wellbeing check-in is done on a teacher’s screen in class.

10. Changes

If we change this notice in an important way, we will tell schools first.
